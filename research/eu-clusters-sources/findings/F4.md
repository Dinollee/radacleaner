## 7. Summary & Recommendations

### What Exists
1. **CEPS** -- Rich policy analysis on enlargement costs, but no live tracker tool
2. **EBRD** -- Extensive Ukraine investment data and Transition Reports, but not accession-specific
3. **Bertelsmann Stiftung** -- Has published on EU enlargement, but no accessible tracker/scoreboard found
4. **GitHub** -- Empty for EU enlargement tracking; no structured data repos exist
5. **Academic/NGO** -- Policy briefs and reports, but no dashboards with structured data

### Key Gap
**There is no open-source, machine-readable tracker for Ukraine EU accession cluster/chapter progress maintained by any think tank, academic institution, or NGO.** All existing sources are:
- PDF-based policy briefs
- Annual reports
- Web pages without APIs

### Best Data Sources for Structured Ingestion
For anyone building a tracker, the most viable data sources are:
1. **EC Enlargement RSS feeds** (covered in F2) -- machine-readable, regularly updated
2. **EBRD project database** -- filterable but not API-accessible
3. **CEPS publication RSS** -- tracks when new enlargement analysis is published
4. **Custom scraping** of EC enlargement pages (see F2 for approach)
