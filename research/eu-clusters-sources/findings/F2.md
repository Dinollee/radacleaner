## 2. API for Negotiation Status Tracking

### Official APIs
**No dedicated public API exists for negotiation status tracking.** The EC does not expose a structured API for cluster/chapter status.

### What is available:

#### a) RSS Feeds (machine-readable, structured)
- **News RSS:** https://enlargement.ec.europa.eu/node/2/rss_en
  - Filter by country using URL params: `?f[0]=country_country:UKR`
  - Example: https://enlargement.ec.europa.eu/news_en?f%5B0%5D=country_country%3Ahttp%3A//publications.europa.eu/resource/authority/country/UKR
- **Documents RSS:** https://enlargement.ec.europa.eu/node/3436/rss_en
- **Country-filtered news URL pattern:**
  `https://neighbourhood-enlargement.ec.europa.eu/about-us/latest-news_en?f[0]=country_country:http://publications.europa.eu/resource/authority/country/UKR`

#### b) data.europa.eu Open Data Portal
- **Search endpoint:** https://data.europa.eu/data/datasets?query=enlargement+ukraine&locale=en
- **API search:** https://data.europa.eu/api/hub/search/
- **SPARQL endpoint:** https://data.europa.eu/data/sparql
- **Note:** No specific enlargement/negotiation dataset found on data.europa.eu as of 2026-07-08. The portal primarily hosts statistical and operational datasets, not negotiation political data.

#### c) EUR-Lex
- **Legal acts search:** https://eur-lex.europa.eu/
- **Ukraine Facility Regulation:** https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=OJ:L_202401449
- **CELEX codes for key documents:**
  - 2024 enlargement communication: CELEX:52024DC0636
- **EUR-Lex SPARQL:** https://data.europa.eu/data/sparql (can query EU legal acts)

#### d) Documents Repository (filterable, but no public API)
- URL: https://enlargement.ec.europa.eu/about-us/documents-repository_en
- Filterable by: country, document theme, date, document type
- Themes include: "Accession negotiations", "Screening Reports", "Negotiating Frameworks"
- **Ponytail:** Scraping this page is feasible -- it's server-rendered HTML with filterable facets.

## 3. Screening Results and Benchmark Data

### Screening Process
- **Screening completed:** September 2025 (announced 30 Sep 2025)
- **News:** https://enlargement.ec.europa.eu/news/ukraine-successfully-completes-its-screening-process-2025-09-30_en
- **Screening reports** are not published individually for each chapter. The screening findings feed into the Negotiating Framework and the State of Play factsheet.

### Where to find screening/benchmark data:
1. **State of Play factsheet (PDF, updated periodically)**
   - Latest: 17 March 2026 (4.41 MB)
   - URL: https://enlargement.ec.europa.eu/document/download/37cd045d-22f4-4fc8-bc6d-c87bb4c7ec9e_en
   - Contains: per-chapter status for all candidate countries (opened, benchmarks, provisional closures)

2. **Ukraine Report 2025 (PDF, 1.91 MB)**
   - URL: https://enlargement.ec.europa.eu/document/download/17115494-8122-4d10-8a06-2cf275eecde7_en
   - Contains: chapter-by-chapter assessment of acquis alignment

3. **2025 Communication on EU Enlargement Policy**
   - URL: https://enlargement.ec.europa.eu/2025-communication-eu-enlargement-policy_en
   - Contains: overall political assessment, cluster openings, recommendations

4. **Chapters of the acquis (reference)**
   - URL: https://enlargement.ec.europa.eu/enlargement-policy/conditions-membership/chapters-acquis_en
   - 35 chapters with descriptions (no status data, just definitions)

5. **Negotiating Framework** (formal Council decision)
   - Published on EUR-Lex when adopted
   - Contains opening/closing benchmarks per chapter

### Cluster Structure (new methodology since 2020)
The 35 acquis chapters are grouped into **6 thematic clusters**:
1. **Fundamentals** (Ch 1, 6, 10, 11, 12, 23, 24, 32) -- rule of law, economic criteria, capacity
2. **Internal Market** (Ch 2, 4, 7, 8, 9, 15, 28, 29)
3. **Competitiveness and inclusive growth** (Ch 13, 14, 16, 17, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22)
4. **Green agenda and sustainable connectivity** (Ch 3, 5, 25, 27)
5. **Resources, agriculture and cohesion** (Ch 30, 33)
6. **External relations** (Ch 31, 34, 35)

Ukraine opened **Cluster 1 (Fundamentals)** on 15 June 2026.

## 4. EUR-Lex and Other Structured Data Sources

### EUR-Lex (Primary source for legal texts)
- **Base URL:** https://eur-lex.europa.eu/
- **Search by CELEX number:** https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=CELEX:XXXXXXXXXX
- **SPARQL endpoint:** https://data.europa.eu/data/sparql
- **Key documents to track:**
  - Enlargement Package Communications (COM/20XX/XXXX)
  - Council Conclusions on Ukraine
  - Negotiating Frameworks
  - Ukraine Facility Regulation (OJ L 2024/1449)
  - Screening reports (not on EUR-Lex -- internal EC documents)
- **EUR-Lex bulk data:** Available via REST API and bulk download (XML/RDF)

## 5. Recommendations for Automated Tracking

### Best approach (no single API exists):
1. **RSS monitoring** -- Subscribe to filtered RSS feeds for Ukraine news and documents
2. **PDF parsing** -- Periodically fetch and parse the State of Play factsheet PDF
3. **News scraping** -- Monitor https://enlargement.ec.europa.eu/news_en with Ukraine filter
4. **EUR-Lex SPARQL** -- Query for new legal acts related to Ukraine accession

### Stable URLs to monitor:
- https://enlargement.ec.europa.eu/countries/ukraine_en (country page, updated with milestones)
- https://enlargement.ec.europa.eu/enlargement-policy/strategy-and-reports_en (annual package)
- https://enlargement.ec.europa.eu/factsheet-state-play-eu-accession-negotiations_en (status factsheet)
- https://enlargement.ec.europa.eu/about-us/documents-repository_en (all official docs)

### Update frequency:
- **Country page:** Updated on major milestones (cluster openings, etc.)
- **Enlargement Package:** Annual (November)
- **State of Play factsheet:** Updated periodically (last: March 2026)
- **News:** Multiple times per week
