## 8. Key Finding: No Single Official "Cluster Tracker" Exists

There is **no single official Ukrainian government page** that lists all 6 clusters and their status in a structured, machine-readable format. The information is scattered across:
- Government press releases (eu-ua.kmu.gov.ua)
- Parliamentary news (rada.gov.ua)
- Media reporting (eurointegration.com.ua)

**Best approach for automated tracking:**
1. Scrape eu-ua.kmu.gov.ua/news/areas/accession-ukraine-to-eu/ for official government positions
2. Monitor pulse.kmu.gov.ua for Association Agreement implementation data
3. Use eurointegration.com.ua as the most reliable real-time source for cluster opening dates
4. Monitor RADA committee news for legislative progress on EU integration bills

**RSS feeds available:**
- RADA: https://www.rada.gov.ua/en/rss/news
- European Pravda likely has RSS (check /eng/ pages)

**Telegram channels:**
- https://www.t.me/EurointegrationComUA (European Pravda)
