# F1: Official VRU website schedule pages

## Findings

### [1] The primary schedule hub is meeting.rada.gov.ua — a full subdomain dedicated to plenary session data with calendar, agenda, voting, and speech endpoints
- quote: "Головна сторінка · Календарний план сесій · Порядок денний засідань · Хронологія розгляду · Результати голосувань · Рейтинг виступаючих · Проходження законопроєктів · Анонси всіх подій"
- url: https://meeting.rada.gov.ua/work/main/index
- source_type: primary
- published: active, accessed 2026-07-08
- confidence: high

### [2] Session calendar plan at meeting.rada.gov.ua/work/main/dayp shows which days are plenary vs question-hour vs work vs holiday, with per-year archives back to 1990
- quote: "Календарний план проведення сесій Верховної Ради України *(п'ятнадцята сесія, IX скликання)* ... Розклад дня: — Година запитань до Уряду ⬤ — Пленарні засідання ⬤"
- url: https://meeting.rada.gov.ua/work/main/dayp
- source_type: primary
- published: active, accessed 2026-07-08
- confidence: high

### [3] Session calendar pages are accessible per-year (e.g. dayp2026) and per-session (e.g. dayp9_15 for 15th session, IX convocation), providing granular date-level plenary scheduling
- quote: "За роками: 2027 ... 2026 ... 2025 ... 2024 ... 2023 ... 2022 ... 2021 ... 2020 ... 2019"
- url: https://meeting.rada.gov.ua/work/main/dayp2026
- source_type: primary
- published: active, accessed 2026-07-08
- confidence: high

### [4] The meeting.rada.gov.ua platform also hosts agendas (/work/main/agenda), voting results (/work/main/voting), and speech rankings (/work/main/speeches) — all critical data sources for calendar integration
- quote: "Порядок денний засідань · Хронологія розгляду порядку денного · Результати голосувань · Візуалізація проходження законопроєктів"

## Dead ends

- `https://opendata.rada.gov.ua` — redirected to `/go`; could not explore available datasets without further navigation. May contain structured schedule data as CSV/JSON but the portal structure needs deeper investigation.
- `https://rada.gov.ua/sched` — returned main homepage, no dedicated /sched route exists.
- `https://rada.gov.ua/sessions` — also returned main homepage, no /sessions route.
- No websearch tool available; relied entirely on direct URL probing of known VRU infrastructure.

## Suggested follow-ups

1. Explore `opendata.rada.gov.ua/go` for structured API endpoints (CSV/JSON/ICS) for voting records and schedules — this may be the most calendar-friendly data source.
2. Fetch a specific weekly committee schedule page (e.g. `http://static.rada.gov.ua/zakon/new/RK/RK010726.htm` for the current week of 29 Jun–3 Jul 2026) to understand the HTML structure and extractable data fields (committee name, date, time, room).
3. Fetch a specific event announcement page (e.g. `https://rada.gov.ua/preview/anonsy_podij/274035.html`) to understand what structured fields are available for committee meeting events (title, time, location, participants).
