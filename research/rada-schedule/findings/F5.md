# F5: Voting session schedules

## Findings

### [1] Voting occurs during plenary sittings — there are no separate "voting sessions"; votes happen as part of the plenary agenda
- quote: "Consideration of agenda items: Course of plenary sittings, Consideration chronology, Consideration results, Electronic protocol, Voting statistics for a given period"
- url: https://www.rada.gov.ua/en/meeting/awt/show/8359.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (page structure is current as of 2026)
- confidence: high

### [2] The official portal provides six distinct schedule-related data sources for plenary sittings: timetable, agenda, weekly committee schedules, consideration chronology, consideration results, and voting statistics
- quote: "Planning and order of consideration: The schedule of sessions of the Parliament, Order of business of a session, Timetable for plenary sittings, Plenary sittings agendas, Weekly schedules for committee sittings"
- url: https://www.rada.gov.ua/en
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [3] Plenary sitting agendas are published on the official portal at rada.gov.ua/en/meeting/awt/show/8359.html
- quote: "Agendas of plenary sessions — Operational information on the results of consideration of agenda items at plenary meetings"
- url: https://www.rada.gov.ua/en/meeting/awt/show/8359.html
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-09-05 (page date)
- confidence: high

### [4] The calendar plan of sessions is published for each session — the current 15th session runs February–July 2026
- quote: "Calendar plan of a session — 15-та сесія Сесія Місяць лютий 2026 — П'ятнадцята сесія Верховної Ради України IX скликання Лютий 2026 року — Липень 2026 року"
- url: https://w2.rada.gov.ua/pls/radan_gs09/ns_el_h
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026 (current session)
- confidence: high

### [5] Bills are usually considered through three readings; voting is an integral part of each reading
- quote: "Bills are usually considered following the procedure of three readings; the President of Ukraine must sign a law before it can be officially promulgated"
- url: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verkhovna_Rada
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2025-09-16
- confidence: high

### [6] The meeting.rada.gov.ua portal provides a dedicated "Voting records" section alongside schedule, agenda, chronology, and speeches
- quote: "Plenary meetings — Main, Shedule, Agenda, Chronology, Voting records, Speeches"
- url: https://meeting.rada.gov.ua/work/main/sps?lang=en
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (current as of 2026)
- confidence: high

### [7] The Rada typically adopts about 200 bills per year, with 5-6 bills registered daily; the voting schedule is driven by how many bills reach plenary consideration
- quote: "In general the parliament adopts about 200 bills per year. An average of five to six bills are registered daily in parliament"
- url: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verkhovna_Rada
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2025-09-16
- confidence: high

### [8] Wartime conditions altered voting patterns: early in the invasion 90% of draft laws were adopted in first reading in under two minutes, but the average time from registration to second-reading adoption has grown to 335 days
- quote: "At the onset of the full-scale invasion... 90% of draft laws were considered in first reading in under two minutes... the average time for consideration to a record-high 335 days — the longest duration observed during the ninth convocation"
- url: https://parlament.org.ua/en/analytics/parliament-of-war-longer-in-wartime-than-in-peace/
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2025-03-26
- confidence: high

### [9] Live broadcasts of plenary sessions (which include voting) resumed in September 2025 after a 3-year wartime ban
- quote: "The Verkhovna Rada will resume broadcasting plenary sessions from mid-September 2025... The resolution was supported by 266 lawmakers"
- url: https://english.nv.ua/nation/ukraine-s-verkhovna-rada-to-resume-broadcasting-of-its-sessions-50542378.html
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2025-09-04
- confidence: high

### [10] The voting system uses electronic voting with individual deputy registration; the Rules of Procedure (last updated December 2012) govern registration and voting procedures
- quote: "All procedural regulations are contained in the Law on Regulations of the Verkhovna Rada of Ukraine. The latest version of the document was adopted on 16 December 2012, in which through the initiative of the President of Ukraine amendments were made concerning registration and voting by parliamentarians"
- url: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verkhovna_Rada
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2025-09-16
- confidence: high

### [11] The official portal publishes "Voting statistics for a given period" as a searchable tool — this is a primary data source for programmatic access to voting data
- quote: "Voting statistics for a given period"
- url: https://www.rada.gov.ua/en/meeting/awt/show/8359.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high
